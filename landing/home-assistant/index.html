ZigDash with Home Assistant

ZigDash shows the devices that Zigbee2MQTT runs. On Home Assistant that means the Mosquitto broker add-on and the Zigbee2MQTT add-on. Your automations stay in Home Assistant and keep running.

Using ZHA? ZHA is Home Assistant's own Zigbee and doesn't use MQTT, so ZigDash can't show those devices. ZigDash is for Zigbee2MQTT.

1. Check the two add-ons

In Home Assistant, open Settings › Add-ons and check that both are installed and started:

  • Mosquitto broker: the MQTT broker.
  • Zigbee2MQTT: with your devices paired. Its configuration should point at the Mosquitto broker (the default on Home Assistant).

2. Make a user for ZigDash

The Mosquitto add-on accepts Home Assistant users. Most people make one just for MQTT:

  1. Settings › People › Users › Add user. (Turn on Advanced mode in your profile if you don't see Users.)
  2. Give it a name like zigdash and a password. It doesn't need to be an administrator.

You can also use your own Home Assistant login, or a login set in the Mosquitto add-on's options.

3. Connect ZigDash

  1. With your phone on your home Wi-Fi, open ZigDash and tap Find my setup. When it asks where Zigbee2MQTT runs, choose Home Assistant.
  2. If it doesn't find it, type the address you open Home Assistant with, for example 192.168.1.20:8123, and tap Check this address. ZigDash finds the broker on the same machine.
  3. When it asks for a login, enter the user from step 2.
homeassistant.local doesn't work on every Android phone. Use the number address: Home Assistant shows it under Settings › System › Network. Don't use your Nabu Casa or DuckDNS link; ZigDash needs the address that works at home.

The Mosquitto add-on checks each login with Home Assistant, which can take a few seconds on a Raspberry Pi. If ZigDash says the broker didn't answer the login, try again.

4. Optional: bring your names and rooms

In ZigDash, Settings › Import from Home Assistant reads the names and rooms you gave your devices in Home Assistant, and can make a dashboard for each room. It reads them once and keeps nothing else from Home Assistant.
