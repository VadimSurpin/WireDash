What it does

Builds itself

Scans your network for the broker, reads your Zigbee2MQTT devices and makes a panel for each one. No topics to type.

Works with Home Assistant

Uses the Mosquitto add-on and signs in with your Home Assistant user.

Without opening the app

Home-screen widgets, Quick Settings tiles and Android Device Controls.

Alerts

A leak, smoke, a door at night or a low battery reaches your phone, even when ZigDash is closed.

Wall tablet

Kiosk mode with fullscreen, dimming and a PIN lock.

Private

Your data stays on your phone and your broker. Anonymous usage data, which you can turn off.

Setting up

  1. Connect your phone to your home Wi-Fi. The scan only finds what is on the same network.
  2. Open ZigDash and tap Find my setup. It looks for your MQTT broker and Zigbee2MQTT.
  3. If it asks for a login, use your broker's user. On Home Assistant, that is your Home Assistant user.
  4. Not found? Type your hub's address (for example 192.168.1.20) and tap Check this address.
