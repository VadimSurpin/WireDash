ZigDash 2.8.1: friendlier setup. When a broker asks for a login, ZigDash now says plainly that it stays on your phone and goes only to your own broker (no server, no account), links what it keeps, and lets you try the demo first. If your phone can see almost nothing on the Wi-Fi, it tells you it may be on a guest network or kept apart by the router. And homes made of sensors now count as happy users too.
