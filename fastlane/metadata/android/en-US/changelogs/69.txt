ZigDash 3.1: Automations and remote control. When a door opens, motion is seen, a button is pressed or a time comes (sunrise and sunset too), ZigDash switches lights, plugs and shutters or runs a scene. ZigDash Engine runs it inside Zigbee2MQTT on your hub, so it works with your phone off. Alerts and history no longer need Node-RED. New in Pro: control your home from anywhere through ZigDash's relay, encrypted end to end. Three automations per home free, unlimited with Pro.
