ZigDash 2.8: bring your Home Assistant home. If you use Home Assistant with Zigbee2MQTT, ZigDash can now take the names and rooms you set up there: sign in on Home Assistant's own login page, check the preview, and your tiles get their names, with a dashboard for each floor and a section for each room. ZigDash only reads, once, and signs out again; your automations keep running in Home Assistant. Find it in Settings › your home, or on the card after setup.
