ZigDash 2.7: history graphs. Tap a reading tile, or History in a device's sheet, to see the last 24 hours of temperature, humidity or power as a graph; touch it to read any moment. A small Node-RED flow on your hub records every number on your dashboards, set up with one tap from the app, and keeps it on your own broker: no cloud, no account. Also: when the scan finds nothing while a VPN is on, ZigDash now says the VPN may be hiding your home network.
