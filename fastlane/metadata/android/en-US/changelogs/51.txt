ZigDash 2.7.2: when the scan finds Zigbee2MQTT but not its broker, ZigDash now asks Zigbee2MQTT where the broker is. If it runs on another address or port, ZigDash connects to it on its own, with Zigbee2MQTT's username filled in. If it only accepts connections from its own machine (Mosquitto 2's default), ZigDash says so and shows the two lines that fix it. Also: "1 device found" instead of "1 devices found".
