ZigDash 2.7.2: easier setup, tested on a real Home Assistant home. Paste your Home Assistant link into the broker form and it just works. Found brokers say what they are: Home Assistant, SMLIGHT hub or Zigbee2MQTT. If Zigbee2MQTT is found but not its broker, ZigDash asks Zigbee2MQTT where it is and connects, or shows the two Mosquitto lines that fix it. Unnamed devices read "Motion sensor f66f", and temperature sensors are ticked for your first dashboard.
