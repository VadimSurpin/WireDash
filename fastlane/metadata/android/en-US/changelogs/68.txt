ZigDash 3.0: Automations. When a door opens, motion is seen, a button is pressed or a time comes (sunrise and sunset too), ZigDash switches lights, plugs and shutters or runs a scene, and can turn them off again. ZigDash Engine runs it all inside Zigbee2MQTT on your hub, installed in one tap, so it works with your phone off and the internet down. Alerts and history no longer need Node-RED. Three automations per home free, unlimited with Pro.
