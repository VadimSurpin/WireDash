ZigDash 2.5: a first run that says what it found. While scanning, tell ZigDash where Zigbee2MQTT runs (Home Assistant, SMLIGHT hub, Pi or Docker) and it guides you. If no MQTT broker answers, the scan looks for Home Assistant or the Zigbee2MQTT page, names it, says the one step that is missing and fills in the address for you. Progress bar while scanning, hints on the manual form. Anonymous usage data is now on by default; untick it on the first screen or turn it off in Settings.
