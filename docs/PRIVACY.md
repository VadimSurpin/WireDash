# WireDash privacy

WireDash has no telemetry SDK, usage tracking, automatic crash reporting, or
feature-request polls. It does not upload app activity to analytics services.
Legacy queued usage events from earlier builds are deleted on upgrade.

Connections, dashboards and settings are stored on your device. Passwords and
WireGuard configuration use encrypted device storage. MQTT, Home Assistant,
WireGuard, and optional alert/history services connect to the services you
configure as needed to provide their features.

Help and problem reports are initiated by you. Support details are shown locally;
copying them or opening an email/report form lets you review what to share.
Opening a community link, an external guide, or a store page uses the chosen
external application and its own privacy policy.
